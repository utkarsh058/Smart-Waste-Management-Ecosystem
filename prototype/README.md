# 🛠️ Physical Hardware Prototype Media & Specs Slot

> **CodersEra Hackathon Prototype Documentation**

This folder contains the hardware circuit diagrams, prototype photos, and operational flowcharts for the **Automatic Waste Segregation & Overflow Monitoring System (2 Containers – Wet & Dry)**.

### Hardware Components List:
1. **IR Sensor**: Item detection at entry funnel.
2. **Capacitive Moisture Sensor v1.2**: Wet vs Dry waste classification.
3. **MG996R High-Torque Servo Motor**: Direction control (Rotate Left for Wet Bin, Rotate Right for Dry Bin).
4. **Ultrasonic Sensor 1 (HC-SR04)**: Measures **Wet Bin** fill level & distance in cm.
5. **Ultrasonic Sensor 2 (HC-SR04)**: Measures **Dry Bin** fill level & distance in cm.
6. **Wet Bin Overflow Alert**: Red LED + Piezo Buzzer ($\ge 85\%$ fill level).
7. **Dry Bin Overflow Alert**: Yellow LED + Piezo Buzzer ($\ge 85\%$ fill level).
8. **ESP32 Microcontroller Board**: Central processing, Wi-Fi & telemetry transmission.
